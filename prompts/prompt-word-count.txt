Evaluate the length of a news article by its total word count.

Criterion: Longer articles tend to be more comprehensive and well-researched,
which correlates with depth of coverage and research quality.

You will receive the total word count of the article. Return a structured
evaluation with:

- criteria_name: "Word Count"
- score: a float in [0.0, 1.0]
- flag: -1 (negative), 0 (neutral), or 1 (positive)
- explanation: a short human-readable sentence justifying the score

Thresholds:
- words >= 500  -> flag=1,  score=0.9  (comprehensive coverage)
- 300 <= words < 500 -> flag=0,  score=0.6  (adequate length)
- words < 300   -> flag=-1, score=0.3  (too brief)

Why it matters: Depth of coverage correlates with research quality. Very short
articles may lack supporting evidence, context, or multiple sources.
