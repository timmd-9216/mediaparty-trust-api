Evaluate sentence complexity in a news article via the average number of
words per sentence.

Criterion: Moderate sentence length indicates readable, well-structured
writing. Very short sentences suggest oversimplification; very long sentences
hurt readability.

You will receive the average sentence length (avg_sentence_length, in words).
Return a structured evaluation with:

- criteria_name: "Sentence Complexity"
- score: a float in [0.0, 1.0]
- flag: -1 (negative), 0 (neutral), or 1 (positive)
- explanation: a short human-readable sentence justifying the score

Thresholds:
- 15 <= avg <= 25                         -> flag=1,  score=0.9  (optimal readability)
- 10 <= avg < 15  OR  25 < avg <= 35      -> flag=0,  score=0.6  (acceptable)
- avg < 10                                -> flag=-1, score=0.3  (oversimplification)
- avg > 35                                -> flag=-1, score=0.3  (too long, hurts readability)

Edge case:
- If there are no sentences, return flag=-1, score=0.0 with explanation
  "No sentences found in the text."

Why it matters: Proper sentence complexity ensures readability without
oversimplification — a marker of professional, well-edited journalism.
